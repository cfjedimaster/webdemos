The Rules of the Serigraph Grid

Every light-cycle leaves an impenetrable solid-state neon ribbon in its wake. Touch the arena border, an opponent’s light ribbon, or your own trail and you instantly DEREZ into geometric confetti. Last rider intact takes the round; first to 3 wins claims the match.

Controls

  • Steer & Boost: Steer with WASD or Arrow Keys. Hold SPACE or SHIFT for Turbo Overdrive.
  • Quick Keys: P Pause • M Toggle Synth/SFX • ENTER Launch Next Round when overlay is open.

Over-The-Top Cyber Mechanics

  • RAZOR GRAZE: Skim within 2 grid cells parallel to any active light wall to throw chrome sparks, rapidly recharge your Overdrive meter, and climb up to a ×8.0 Style Multiplier.
  • GOLD OVERDRIVE PRISM: Instant 100% Overdrive refill + bonus points.
  • CRIMSON SLASH PRISM: Vaporizes the oldest 40% of every bike’s light trail across the grid, opening lethal ambush corridors.
  • TEAL CHRONO PRISM: Triggers 3 seconds of New-Wave Time Dilation—enemy cycles crawl at half speed while you carve full-speed circles around them.
